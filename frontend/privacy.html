01

Overview

Content Machine ("we", "us", "our") operates an AI-powered social media content management platform. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

🛡️ We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We collect only what is necessary to operate the Service.

02

Data We Collect

Category What We Collect Purpose
Account Data Username, hashed password Authentication & access control
Social Tokens OAuth access tokens for connected platforms Publishing content on your behalf
Content Data Scraped articles, AI-generated posts, carousel images Core Service functionality
Usage Data API request logs, scheduled jobs, error logs Service operation & debugging
Configuration Data Source URLs, platform settings, templates Personalizing your pipeline

We do not collect: payment information, precise geolocation, biometric data, or data from minors.

03

How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect to:

  • Authenticate you and provide access to the platform
  • Ingest, process, and generate content from your configured sources
  • Publish approved content to your connected social media accounts
  • Schedule and execute publishing jobs on your behalf
  • Maintain logs for debugging and service reliability
  • Improve the platform based on usage patterns (aggregated, anonymized)

We do not use your data to train our AI models or for advertising purposes.

04

Social Media Tokens

When you connect a social media account (LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, X, Snapchat, Threads), we receive and store OAuth access tokens. These tokens:

  • Are stored securely in our database, encrypted at rest
  • Are used exclusively to publish content you have approved
  • Are never shared with third parties outside the platform's own API
  • Can be revoked at any time by disconnecting the account in settings or via the social platform directly

⚠️ Revoking a token from the social platform will disconnect it from Content Machine. We do not store your social platform passwords.

05

AI & Content Processing

Content Machine uses Claude (Anthropic) as its AI backbone. When generating content:

  • Article text and titles are sent to the AI API for content generation
  • We send only the minimum context needed (no personal account data)
  • AI-generated content is stored in our database until deleted by you
  • Anthropic's own privacy policy governs how they handle API inputs

Generated carousel images are stored on Cloudinary and linked in your content pipeline. You can delete them at any time through the platform.

06

Third-Party Services

Content Machine integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Anthropic (Claude API) — AI content generation
  • Cloudinary — Image storage and delivery
  • Neon (PostgreSQL) — Managed database hosting
  • Railway — Application hosting and deployment
  • Social Platform APIs — LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, X, Snapchat, Threads

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review them. We only share data with these services as necessary to operate Content Machine.

07

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service:

  • Published content records are retained for analytics and history
  • Rejected or deleted content is removed from the active pipeline
  • OAuth tokens are deleted when you disconnect a social account
  • Account data is retained until you request deletion

To request complete data deletion, contact us using the information below.

08

Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data in transit
  • Encrypted storage for sensitive credentials (OAuth tokens, API keys)
  • Authentication required for all platform actions
  • Regular dependency updates to address known vulnerabilities

No system is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach affecting your personal data, we will notify affected users in accordance with applicable law.

09

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion — Request deletion of your personal data
  • Portability — Request your data in a portable format
  • Revocation — Revoke OAuth permissions for any connected platform at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information in Section 12.

10

Cookies & Local Storage

Content Machine uses minimal browser storage:

  • Session tokens — Stored in localStorage to maintain your login session
  • We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking pixels
  • We do not use Google Analytics or similar tracking services

You can clear your browser's local storage at any time to log out of the platform.
